About distances & crossing times

Each milestone shows two different distances: the on-foot distance along our mapped route and the straight-line distance from Meriden. The on-foot figure is not an actual GPS watch recording; it's estimated from the planned route reused from 2025 for this 2026 event. Marathon is 42.195 km along that route. Silver and gold are 30 and 60 miles as the crow flies; only the official tracker decides the awards. Crossing locations and times are approximate. Times are UK local.
